**One-line summary:** The lab combines hydro-climate science (droughts, heatwaves, water and carbon fluxes) with systems engineering (optimization, decision support, AI). Its newest and fastest-growing thread is siting AI data centers and assessing their energy, water, and land footprint in Tennessee.

## 2. Research Directions

### 2.1 AI Data Center Siting & Infrastructure Planning (new core direction)
- Site selection and capacity allocation for AI data centers under power, water, land, network, and policy constraints.
- Uncertainty: interconnection queues, demand growth, electricity supply scenarios, shifting tariffs and moratoria.
- Local footprint: freshwater withdrawals, thermal discharges, noise, land-market effects; data centers co-located with small modular reactors (SMRs).
- Methods: robust / stochastic optimization, scenario analysis, geospatial analysis, ML-assisted optimization, NLP for policy and requirement extraction.
- Evidence: ISSE grant on the local footprint of AI data centers (funded 2026) and the USGS 104b TDEC decision-support grant (funded 2027). Ongoing papers by Yifan Gao and Shi Jin, plus a submitted paper on water use tied to data-center electricity demand.

### 2.2 Climate Extremes & Ecohydrology (PI's established expertise)
- Flash droughts, heatwaves, flash floods; ecosystem recovery after drought; water-use efficiency.
- Evapotranspiration (ET) estimation and partitioning (Priestley–Taylor, complementary relationship, Bayesian Model Averaging).
- Soil moisture controls on carbon sequestration and greenhouse gas emissions; terrestrial water storage.
- Data: remote sensing (MODIS, multi-source), AmeriFlux / flux towers, drought indices.

### 2.3 Earth System Modeling & Ecosystem Vulnerability
- Collaboration with Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL): E3SM regionally refined configuration; Compound Indicators for Vulnerable Ecosystems (CIVE v1.0) framework.
- Boreal and temperate ecosystem vulnerabilities and model uncertainty (DOE/ORNL grant, PI).

### 2.4 Decision Support for Agriculture, Land & Energy Systems
- Remote-sensing-based decision-support tools for flash droughts and floods (FFAR grant, Co-PI); irrigation planning under stochastic optimization.
- Carbon removal and land management: enhanced rock weathering, cover cropping, precision poplar afforestation on marginal and fragipan soils.
- Wildfire–power grid interactions; fire risk in the humid Southeastern US.
