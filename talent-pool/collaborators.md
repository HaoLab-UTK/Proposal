recommended in funding scans, maintained under the "Collaborator Rule" in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md). Only
public, professional information is recorded (no contact details). Status is
"Past collaborator" for people who already published with the PI, and
"Recommended" for scan suggestions until the user changes it to "Contacted"
or "Confirmed". No entry means the person has agreed to join a proposal.
