# Collaborator Registry

Candidate co-PIs and partner organizations recommended in funding scans,
maintained under the "Collaborator Rule" in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md). Only
public, professional information is recorded (no contact details). Status is
"Recommended" until the user changes it to "Contacted" or "Confirmed"; no
entry means the person has agreed to anything.
