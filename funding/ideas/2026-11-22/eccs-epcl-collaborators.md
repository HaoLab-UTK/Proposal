## Q3. Who should join a Hao Lab-led EPCL proposal (co-PIs and partners)

### Takeaway
The proposal stands or falls on a power-systems co-PI, because the lab has
none. Two UT Knoxville EECS faculty are the most practical choices: Hector
Pulgar holds an EPCN CAREER award (new to the registry), and Fangxing Fran Li
directs CURENT and is a past co-author (in the registry). Ten people are
ranked below, eight of them new to the registry, so the more-than-half-new
requirement is met. Nobody has been contacted and none has agreed to
anything. No minority-serving-institution candidate with relevant power
expertise was found (see Gaps). No candidate's gender was considered,
because none was found stated on the candidates' own public profiles.

### Inferences
- **Recommended structure for P1.** Hao as PI (or Pulgar as PI and Hao as co-PI; ask the program team which reads better to EPCL panels). Pulgar or Li as co-PI. Sims as senior personnel. ORNL NGDCI and TVA letters. Fan or Taha as unfunded collaborators.
- **Recommended structure for P2.** Hao as PI (with students Shi Jin and Yifan Gao), Sims as co-PI (economics) and Pulgar or Li as co-PI (power), plus Jiang as methods advisor.
- **Differentiation from registry use.** CLaSH recommendations (2026-11-20) were all geoscience. This list adds power, controls, economics and national-lab grid people for the first time, which is the expertise K1, C2 and E1 also flagged as missing ("a power-systems Co-PI is needed").
