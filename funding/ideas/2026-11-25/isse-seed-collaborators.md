Research notes for the 2026-11-25 scan (Collaborator Rule). Only public,
professional information is used. No one listed here has been contacted or has

## Key Question 3: Co-PIs and partner organizations for the ISSE 2027 seed ideas (IS1 to IS3)

### Takeaway
Eight of the twelve recommended people are new to the registry, which meets the
more-than-half-new rule. Seven of them are UT Knoxville faculty in CEE,
EECS/CURENT and UTIA Biosystems Engineering and Soil Science; the eighth is a
Vanderbilt resilient-infrastructure researcher. Five of the seven UT people are
past or current ISSE seed PIs or co-PIs. The highest-value additions are:
- Joshua S. Fu (climate impacts on energy infrastructure; ORNL joint appointment), co-PI for IS1.
- Kai Sun (CURENT; past ISSE seed PI on the grid effects of hydrogen loads), PI for IS2.
- Haochen Li and Jon Hathaway (FY27 ISSE flood seed), leads for IS3.
- Emine Fidan (UTIA; past ISSE seed PI on water availability), co-PI for IS1.

Partner organizations: ORNL's Southeast Resilience Accelerator, TVA, the TDEC
Office of Energy Programs, the City of Knoxville and TNWRRC. I found no suitable
Baker School, nuclear engineering or UTIA poplar person beyond those already in
the registry; reasons are under Gaps.

### Inferences
- **Best team per idea.**
  - IS1: Hao (PI) + Fu + Fidan, with ORNL (Mao; SERA contact via Kate Evans) and TVA data partners, and TNWRRC (Schwartz) as advisor.
  - IS2: Kai Sun (PI) + Hao, with Tolbert optional, CURENT, TVA and TDEC Office of Energy Programs.
  - IS3: Hathaway or Li (PI) + Hao + Ellis, with the City of Knoxville. Hold Baroud for an external proposal letter, since a non-UT person cannot be an ISSE PI.
- **Diversity of background.** The new people bring other disciplines (power systems, climate and air-quality modeling, fluid mechanics, urban water, agricultural water), other units (UTIA, ORNL, Vanderbilt), industry experience (Kai Sun at EPRI) and early-career stages (Li and Fidan are assistant professors). No gender characteristics are inferred or recorded.
- **Load across calls.** Kai Sun, Fu, Li, Hathaway and Fidan are not recommended in any other tracked call, so they spread the load away from Sims, Mao and Mingzhou Jin, who appear in several.

Fu, Kai Sun, Haochen Li, Jon Hathaway, Emine Fidan, John Schwartz, Hongyu (Nick) Zhou, Hiba Baroud and Kate Evans; organizations ORNL SERA, TDEC Office of Energy Programs, TNWRRC, City of Knoxville and the UT ORIED AI Tennessee Initiative.
