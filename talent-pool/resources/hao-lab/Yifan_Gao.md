## Data Center Siting and Infrastructure Planning
*Planned Doctoral Research | Geospatial Analysis, Optimization*

- Plan to develop a data-driven framework for data center site selection, integrating geospatial analysis with optimization.
- Investigate how energy availability, network connectivity, land characteristics, and environmental risks influence location decisions.
- Explore methods for selecting sites and allocating capacity under resource constraints and competing objectives.
- **Example project:** Identify suitable data center locations within a region and evaluate alternative configurations under budget and power capacity constraints.

## Infrastructure Planning Under Uncertainty
*Prospective Research | Robust Optimization, Scenario Analysis*

- Explore how infrastructure location and capacity decisions respond to changes in computing demand, energy supply, and environmental conditions.
- Investigate planning approaches that account for uncertainty and long-term infrastructure resilience.
- **Example project:** Develop a data center planning model that evaluates location and expansion strategies across future demand and electricity supply scenarios.

## Machine Learning for Optimization
*Research Interest | Machine Learning, Combinatorial Optimization*

- Investigate how machine learning can improve the efficiency of complex siting and resource allocation problems.
- Explore learning-based candidate screening, initial solution generation, and optimization heuristics.
- **Example project:** Develop and evaluate a learning-assisted approach to large-scale data center site selection, comparing solution quality and computational performance with conventional methods.

## Natural Language Processing for Decision Support
*Undergraduate Research Background & Future Interest | NLP, Information Extraction*

- Build on undergraduate NLP research experience to explore the use of unstructured text in infrastructure planning.
- Investigate methods for extracting requirements and constraints from policy documents, planning reports, and technical materials.
- **Example project:** Construct a source-linked dataset of data center development requirements and assess how information extraction errors affect downstream siting decisions.

## Distributed Computing and AI Workload Scheduling
*Research Interest | Computing Systems, Resource Allocation*

- Explore workload placement and scheduling across geographically distributed data centers.
- Investigate trade-offs among computing costs, network latency, resource availability, and task completion requirements.
- **Example project:** Design scheduling strategies for AI training and inference workloads across multiple locations, accounting for their different resource and latency requirements.
