**Correction for DOE Office of Science, DE-FOA-0003665 (BER).** Per the Part 1
NOFO (Version 4.1), BER's FY2027 review panels cover only three topics:
Microbial Biosystems Design, Classical and Quantum-enabled Bioimaging and
Sensing, and Low Dose Radiation Research. Pre-applications for those panels are
due 2026-12-22. Earth system or ecosystem modeling is not a panel topic. Such
work can still be submitted at any time as "research within BER's
above-described mission space" outside the panel topics; it is then evaluated
under the NOFO's general criteria. The NOFO stays open until 2027-09-30. The
2026-11-30 pre-application date in the 2026-10-21 scan comes from other Office
of Science programs, not BER. Contact the BER program manager before writing.
