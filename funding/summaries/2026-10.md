## 4. Overlap and duplication rules

The same theme (data center siting and water under compound flash drought and
heat in the Tennessee Valley) fits most of these calls. Each call was given its
own question so the proposals do not duplicate one another or the lab's funded
USGS 104b TDEC, UT ISSE, and FFAR projects:

- NSF: do not have EWRE E1, CMMI-EBE C2, and CAREER K1 pending at the same
  time with the same core; C2 is on hold unless CAREER is not possible.
- NOAA: duplication with other federal projects is a selection factor; N1
  must stay observational threshold science, not siting or facility design.
- USGS 104b TDEC grant: SMR thermal-discharge decision tools stay there.
- List all pending proposals in Current and Pending Support.
