## Who should join a Sloan E&E letter of inquiry led or co-led by Hao Lab

### Takeaway
The lab's gap for this call is social science: energy economics, political science and public administration, sociology or geography, and energy law. The collaborator registry (`talent-pool/collaborators.md`) holds only Earth system modelers, hydrologists, an optimization co-author and a power-grid co-author. Nine of the ten recommended people are therefore **new**, well over half. Five are Sloan E&E grantees in the same program: Ambrose, Schwartzman, Luke, Nguyen and Verma (Verma only as a coordination contact). The first choice for SL1 is energy economist **Charles B. Sims** (UT Baker School and Economics), who adds TVA advisory experience at the same institution. **Gabe Schwartzman** and **Nikki Luke** (UT Knoxville, on the $1M Emory/UTK Sloan data center grant) should be approached first, as coordination partners or advisors, not as co-PIs on a competing grant.

### Inferences
- **For SL1:** Sims (economics co-lead) + Plank (LPC practitioner) + Chen or Luke (energy burden) + Mingzhou Jin (optimization), with Schwartzman as advisor. This mirrors the funded Sloan pattern: social-science lead, engineering collaborator, practitioner advisory board, student training.
- **For SL3:** Ambrose or Basseches as co-lead, with the NLC tracker as seed data.
- **Diversity of backgrounds:**
  - Disciplines: economics, geography, public administration, political science/sociology, energy justice.
  - Institution types: an HBCU (NC A&T), a regional comprehensive campus (UTC), a municipal utility (EPB), a private research university (CWRU).
  - Career stages: four assistant professors, research faculty, a senior professor.
  - Regions: Tennessee, North Carolina, Ohio, Michigan.
- **Overlap management:** Schwartzman, Luke and Verma are recommended mainly to prevent duplication with funded Sloan projects. Their participation should be decided after a conversation, and their funded work must not be claimed as a team asset.
