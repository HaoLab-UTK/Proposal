# ORAU Powe Junior Faculty Enhancement Awards (FY2027): recommended collaborators and partner organizations

Research notes for the 2026-11-22 scan (Collaborator Rule). The Powe award is single-PI: the applicant is Yuefeng Hao, and students can be paid. The people below would be asked only for data access, a letter of collaboration or support, or informal mentoring. None would be a co-PI. Only public, professional information is used. No one has been contacted, and no one has agreed to anything. Registry checked: `talent-pool/collaborators.md` (as of 2026-11-22).

## Key Question 3: Who could strengthen the Powe proposal (PW1 lead, PW2 fallback)?

### Takeaway
Eight people are recommended. Six are new to the registry (more than half) and two come from it. Five organizations are also recommended. The proposal's weakest criterion is the applicant's record on nuclear materials and SMRs, so the highest-weighted people fill that gap:
- the ORNL OR-SAGE siting team (Femi Omitaomu, Randy Belles);
- the UT Knoxville researchers already studying SMR + hyperscale data center microgrids (Leon M. Tolbert, Jamie Coble).

UT Baker School energy-policy faculty (Charles Sims, Timothy Fitzgerald) add a different discipline for the deployment-policy framing. The most important organization is UT Knoxville's ORAU Councilor office and ORIED limited-submissions office, which controls the two nominations.

### Inferences
- **PW1:** one letter from the ORNL OR-SAGE side (Omitaomu) and one from UT Nuclear Engineering (Coble) or CURENT (Tolbert) would most improve the "applicant competence" and "facilities and resources" scores. A two-page Powe proposal has room to mention them, though letters beyond the chair/dean letter are not listed as required items. Whether extra letters can be uploaded is not stated, so check PeerNet or ask ORAU. The proposal can instead name collaborations in the text, which the FAQ encourages.
- **Mentors:** Mingzhou Jin and Jiafu Mao are natural mentors who can support the chair/dean nomination, but they do not cover the nuclear-materials gap.
- **PW2:** Jiafu Mao (ORNL) and the OR-SAGE team cover the needs; Tolbert's microgrid project gives the operations context.
