## Key question: Who should co-lead or partner on the Ecosystem Science proposals (BE1 lead, BE2/BE3 Revise), and why?

### Takeaway
Ten people are recommended: **8 new** (not in the registry) and 2 from the registry, so more than half are new. The most important gap is an ecologist co-PI who has won Ecosystem Science or MacroSystems/NEON awards; Stephanie Kivlin (UT Knoxville, same institution) and Elizabeth LaRue (UTEP, NEON structural diversity) fit BE1 best, with Kimberly Novick (eastern forest flux, current Ecosystem Science award) as senior collaborator. Organizations: NEON Domain 07 sites, AmeriFlux, USDA Forest Service Coweeta Hydrologic Laboratory, Smithsonian ForestGEO/SCBI, ORNL.

### Inferences
- **Minimum team for BE1:** Hao (PI) + Kivlin (UT co-PI) + LaRue (co-PI, collaborative subaward) + Novick or Johnson (hydraulics/flux senior collaborator). This gives an ecologist-majority team with ES and MacroSystems award histories, which matches the past-winner pattern.
- **EPSCoR option:** a collaborative led from Iowa (Dannenberg) or Mississippi (Siegert) would be "particularly welcomed," but it would make Hao Lab a non-lead partner; keep UT as lead unless program officers advise otherwise.
- **Overlap to manage:** Dannenberg's SCALE-UP and Teixeira's LTREB are current awards in the same program; their roles must add data or expertise without duplicating those projects' aims.
