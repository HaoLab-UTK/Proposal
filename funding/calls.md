### Call Notes

**USGS 104g General FY2026 (G27AS00164)** — the most urgent and best-matched call.
- The FY2026 general competition targets data center water use: observing,
  estimating, validating, and forecasting water use by data centers and other
  water-intensive industries (withdrawals, cooling demand, local impacts), with
  AI/ML methods encouraged.
- Fits Hao Lab's ISSE data center footprint project and the USGS 104b TDEC
  decision-support grant; Yifan Gao and Shi Jin's siting models supply the
  demand side.
- Only State Water Resources Research Institutes submit to Grants.gov.
  Tennessee applicants go through the Tennessee Water Resources Research
  Center (tnwrrc.tennessee.edu), whose internal deadline was not found and
  will be before 2026-11-12. Contact the center immediately.
- 1:1 non-federal cost share required; up to 3-year projects; anticipated start
  2027-05-01.

**DOE Office of Science FY2027 continuation (DE-FOA-0003665)**
- Umbrella solicitation covering all Office of Science programs, including
  Biological and Environmental Research (BER). Fits the PI's E3SM / CIVE
  ecosystem-vulnerability work with ORNL.
- A pre-application is required for some review panels; check Part 1 and the
  BER topic list for an Earth and Environmental Systems Sciences topic before
  writing.

**NSF GEO Core (NSF 26-516)**
- Replaced the archived Hydrologic Sciences and WaLCZ programs as of
  2026-09-26; hydrology now goes to the Earth Sciences program within GEO Core.
- Fits flash drought, ET partitioning, soil moisture, and ecosystem recovery
  research.

**NSF CBET EWRE (NSF 26-518)**
- Energy-water-resource engineering; candidate home for data center water and
  energy footprint work. Confirm scope fit with a program director.

**NSF CMMI (NSF 26-515)**
- The former Operations Engineering program page is archived; operations
  research now sits mainly in Advanced Manufacturing, Design, and Operations.
  Robust / stochastic siting under interconnection-queue and tariff
  uncertainty fits better as an infrastructure-systems problem. Confirm with a
  program director.

**NSF CAREER**
- Topic-agnostic, so it is listed on PI eligibility: the PI became an
  Assistant Professor in 2026-08. One proposal per PI per year, no co-PIs,
  and eligibility is judged on the deadline date. A data center siting plus
  water-footprint research and education plan would build on the lab's
  strongest current thread.
